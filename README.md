# Problem to solve

Patients suffering from Alzheimer's and dementia struggle with remembering important information about their friends and family. Even if they have known each other for a long time, they cannot keep a strong bond with their loved ones as every conversation is completely fresh.



# Our solution

ForgetMeNot is an XR-based solution that remembers people based off a facial recognition hashing and uses LLMs and Speech-to-Text to store important information about them. Users can record their conversations through the Meta Quest and summarize them to retain context on their relationships when encountering someone they met again. Thus, they can jump back into the conversation and consistently rebuild lost connections.
